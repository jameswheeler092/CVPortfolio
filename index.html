Guardian Compliance

A purpose-built compliance solution for the land-based gambling industry. The system handled sensitive personal data, regulatory returns, and compliance requirements for the regulated sector.

950

sites live as of 19 October 2026

35%

of the sites in the market

Problem

Operators kept customer records on paper or in legacy systems that weren’t fit for purpose. They needed a quick, affordable way to talk to customers, review behaviour, escalate potential vulnerability and show an audit trail across venues.

Build

I was the end-to-end product manager, shaping it with the trade association and pilot customers as regulation evolved. One customer record spans every venue. It connects to facial recognition, self-exclusion schemes and our machine management system, so gameplay alerts prompt staff to step in on AML, fraud and safer gambling.

Outcome

Unseated the market incumbent’s largest customer, and sub-licensed the solution to the trade body for use across its membership base.
