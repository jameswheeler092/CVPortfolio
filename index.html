RBW Atlas

Internal AI Native company operating system

The problem

RBW ran on paper and memory. Data was scattered across personal devices, Excel sheets, third-party software, and pretty much any place you could imagine. The team had no central source of truth or foundations to evolve the business as we scaled.

The build

As we looked at the rapid advancements in AI, we recognised the need to evolve our business, the first stage being creating a single system to centralise core functions and data. We opted to create a solid foundation of data and functionality before implementing AI workflows over the top.

The outcome

A single system covering CRM, onboarding, installations & inventory, customer support, QBRs, employee hub, server telemetry, and an AI agent with custom knowledge bases, telemetry, multiple models, and observability.

Guardian Compliance

A purpose-built compliance solution for the land-based gambling industry. The system handled sensitive personal data, regulatory returns, and compliance requirements for the regulated sector.

The problem

Operators kept customer records on paper or in legacy systems that weren’t fit for purpose. They needed a quick, affordable way to talk to customers, review behaviour, escalate potential vulnerability and show an audit trail across venues.

The build

I was the end-to-end product manager; working with stakeholders from the industry trade association, and pilot customers to shape the product around the evolving regulatory landscape and business needs. The system was architected around a singular customer record across every venue, where staff can record interactions, KYC checks, and escalate vulnerability where needed. Integrated the system with facial recognition solutions, self-exclusion schemes, and our own machine management system to prompt staff intervention based on automatic gameplay alerts, covering both AML and fraud, and safer gambling concerns.

The outcome

950 sites live as of 19 October 2026, 35% of the sites in the market. Unseated the market incumbent’s largest customer, and sub-licensed the solution to the trade body for use across its membership base.
